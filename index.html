04 / Why this exists

Not just minutes.
A point of view.

THE BROOK'S MUN DIARIES is a student-led space for documenting Model United Nations with clarity, curiosity and care. The aim is to make debate accessible without flattening the nuance or pretending every question has an easy answer.

The newsroom rules
VerifyCheck facts, names and claims before publication.
Be fairRepresent positions accurately and in context.
Own itCorrect mistakes transparently and promptly.
